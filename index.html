Our Story

Born in a small studio, built on patience.

RELAXX began with a handful of sewing machines and one conviction: clothes should be made to last. We still cut in small runs, so nothing is made without a reason.

Honest fibres, finished by hand.

Organic cotton, washed linen and responsibly sourced wool, sewn by the same small workshops we have worked with since day one. Pieces that soften with wear, made to be kept, repaired and worn for years.
